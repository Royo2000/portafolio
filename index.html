About me

I'm Rodrigo, a video editor and filmmaker from Puebla, Mexico. I studied Communication at IBERO Puebla and I'm now doing an MFA in Filmmaking at CINEMA.

Since 2023 I've worked remotely with US teams: first as a digital storyteller for Living Water Ministries in Michigan, filming camp programs on site and editing the footage for campaigns and fundraising, and since March 2026 as a video editor for Vedenmedia, an Atlanta-based agency, cutting social promos, interviews and long-form videos for commercial clients.

At university I also produced weekly Reels for IBERO's institutional channels, and did studio photography, motion graphics and graphic design. My graduation project, Entre Sombras, is a short-film teaser backed by a research manual on using AI inside a real production workflow.

I work in English and Spanish, edit in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and After Effects, and I'm available for full-time or contract remote work.
